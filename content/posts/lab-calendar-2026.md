Since 2024, the Graduate Student Association in the Section of Natural Resources (our home at Cornell) has been doing
an annual calendar. It's simple, lab groups and staff submit a photo that will be featured on the calendar. The point
is to both showcase the faces of NRE, but also draw a sense of community through whimsy. Many of the images in the
calendar are photoshopped to be delightfully (and playfully) awful. Over the last two years, the Therkildsen Lab's creative
approached has been "album covers." While there's still pleny of room to grow (e.g., NSYNC's No Strings Attached, Beatle's Abbey Road),
_I_ think we're on a hot streak. Here's a look at our fun calendar photos.

### 2024
The idea here was to combine laboratory safety and pipetting with the simple act of adding creamer to coffee,
with all the endearing body language of stock photos for "laboratory" and "science." This was our first attempt,
and I like to believe we've refined our approach since.

![calendar photo 2024](/images/posts/labcalphoto2024.jpg)

### 2025
There was no specific album in mind for this one, but we went for a general 'gritty hip hop' tone here.
The photo that was chosen ended up being the last one we took as a "hey, since we're all here, everyone
huddle in" picture. This set the precedent for trying to submit photos that pretended to be album covers. 

![calendar photo 2025](/images/posts/labcalphoto2025.jpg)

### 2026

90s or early 2000s. The first draft of this was a reference to New Kids on the Block "Therkilds on the 
Block", which was pretty niche, but it looked _pretty good_. The day of submission, I, a huge fan of
Ace of Base from my childhood, realized it would be a much better play on words, since we _do_ work with
