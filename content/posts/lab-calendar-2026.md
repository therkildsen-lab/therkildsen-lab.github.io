Since 2024, the Graduate Student Association in the Section of Natural Resources (our home at Cornell) has been doing
an annual calendar. It's simple, lab groups and staff submit a photo that will be featured on the calendar. The point
is to both showcase the faces of NRE, but also draw a sense of community through whimsy. Many of the images in the
calendar are photoshopped to be delightfully (and playfully) awful. Over the last two years, the Therkildsen Lab's creative
approached has been "album covers." While there's still pleny of room to grow,
_I_ think we're getting better each year. Here's a look at our fun calendar photos.

### 2024
This was the inagural year for the NRE calender. We posted several ideas, but were largely limited by everyone's busy
schedules (unsurprisingly). The idea we eventually landed on was to combine laboratory safety and pipetting with the
simple act of adding creamer to coffee, with all the endearing body language of stock photos for "laboratory" and "science."
This was how we were immortalized on the first NRE calendar,and I like to believe we've refined our approach since.

![calendar photo 2024](/images/posts/labcalphoto2024.jpg)

### 2025
Schedules really didn't get any more flexible, which is to be expected-- science is busy work! So, the
idea was to do something without props and with less staging. I'm not sure when the idea of an album
cover came up, but that's what we ended up doing. There was no specific album in mind for this one, but
it was all about the poses, thinking a bit of photoshop will make up for the rest. In the end, we went for
a general 'gritty hip hop' tone. The photo that was chosen was actually the last one we took, when Nina suggested
we take one more since everyone was already gathered together and just put the camera underneath us. It was our 2025
submission that set the precedent for trying to do photos that pretended to be album covers. 

![calendar photo 2025](/images/posts/labcalphoto2025.jpg)

### 2026

90s or early 2000s, although I did suggest we try [Heaven and Hell (Black Sabbath)](https://www.black-sabbath.com/images/covers/heavenandhell_large.jpg)
and [The Album (BLACKPINK)](https://dbkpop.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/blackpink_the_album_jp_version_cover_all.jpg), and both ideas were unsurprisingly ignored.
