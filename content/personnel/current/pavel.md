Pavel is interested in how fish movement over evolutionary time impacts and informs conservation genetics.
His experience has been in highly migratory fishes in marine systems (sharks, tunas) and he has turned his
attention to the anadromous American shad. He wrote the linked-read software Harpy, Djinn, Mimick, took over
Lariat and rebranded it Arachne, and seems to generally name things after monsters now. Pavel currently works
as a postdoc in the EGCF with Nick (Locatelli, below), but Nina won't let him move this blurb to the alumni section yet.
